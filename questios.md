Purpose: What is the website’s main goal and target audience?
Informing people already involved with the project (people who we refer to the website), and people who want to be involved with the lab (colleagues and researchers who we already know). Target audience = students, parents, principals, teachers, researchers (potentially practitioners in the future).

Structure and features: One-page or multi-page? Do you need research, news, team profiles, publications, vacancies, forms, or search?
See website requirements. Multipage for big sections, one page inside the big section. About us section first, after opening the website. Search is not needed. We might want to add “animations” or “simulations” later.

Future plans: What might you want to add later?
Add new sections, subsections, edit existing sections (add text, pictures, videos, pdf). Ability to trasform big section “Parta” into subsection “projects” later. Is it possible to add new people icons and news icons (see https://inpsy.fss.muni.cz/vyzkum/big-lab)?

Languages: Which languages are needed now?
Czech, English. Can we add other languages later? Can we translate only some sections or do we need whole webpage?

Design: Which websites do you like, and why? Are there university branding guidelines?
See website requirements. No need to follow university branding.

Materials: What already exists: website, texts, photos, logo? Who provides missing content and translations?
We have one webpage https://inpsy.fss.muni.cz/vyzkum/big-lab. We want to source content “people” “about lab” and “publications” “ collaboration”. Missing content and translations will be provided by us.

Content editing: What should you be able to update yourself, and how often?
See “3. Future plans”. About once a month.

Editors: Who will manage the content? Should someone approve changes before publication?
We want to manage the content ourselves. No one needs to approve the changes. We only require one access (admin / account) for the whole lab.

Technology: Does the university require a specific website platform, such as Umbraco (the content management system I found listed on the university’s website), or can we choose another platform, such as Django/Wagtail?
We do not require any platform, however we can grant access to it. Use whatever is best for the website requirements.

Hosting and deployment: Will the website run on university servers or external hosting? Who arranges hosting and provides access to publish the site?
External hosting. We are ready to pay for it, however, we need your help with setting everything up.

Domain: Is there an existing domain? Will the university provide a subdomain, or do we need to register a new domain? Who owns and manages it?
We prefer registering new one. We are ready to pay for it, however, we need your help with setting everything up.

Migration and integrations: Should we transfer existing content, preserve old links, or import publications and profiles automatically?
See “6. Materials”.

University requirements: Are there accessibility, privacy, or other requirements we need to follow?
No need. The purpose of the web is to inform. There will be no accounts or personal data of users.

Maintenance: Who handles updates, backups, technical issues, and recurring costs after launch?
Updating information will be handled by lab members. We will pay for recurring costs. Can potential technical issues be handled by you later?
